# al-Fawāʾid al-Ḍiyāʾiyya (Sharḥ Mullā Jāmī)

**Download (latest release):** [Manuscript PDF](https://github.com/maronesy/translation/releases/latest/download/Mulla-Jami_Manuscript.pdf) ·
[Word](https://github.com/maronesy/translation/releases/latest/download/Mulla-Jami_Translation.docx) · [Review notes](https://github.com/maronesy/translation/releases/latest/download/Mulla-Jami_Review-Notes.md)

- **Arabic title:** الفوائد الضيائية، المشهور بملا جامي
- **Author:** Nūr al-Dīn ʿAbd al-Raḥmān al-Jāmī (d. 898/1492). A commentary on
  *al-Kāfiya* of Ibn al-Ḥājib, on Arabic grammar (*naḥw*).
- **Edition followed:** *al-Fawāʾid al-Ḍiyāʾiyya al-mashhūr bi-Mullā Jāmī*,
  اعتنى به إلياس قيلان. Istanbul: Şifa Yayınevi, 2015. ISBN 978-605-84063-1-5.
  The matn of the commentary is printed with numbered ḥawāshī beneath it; both
  are transcribed and translated. The scan is not included.
- **Status:** complete — book pp. ٢٣–٥٩٩, from Jāmī's opening to his colophon
  (dated Saturday, 11 Ramaḍān 897). The indices that follow are not
  translated.
- **Gap:** book p. ٢٨ is missing from the scan used (a blank leaf). A
  contributor with the printed book can supply it as `pages/page_029.py`.
- **Arabic:** written without diacritics (`diacritics = false`).
- **Scan page = book page + 1.**

[REVIEW-NOTES.md](REVIEW-NOTES.md) lists, by book page, the places where the
edition looks misprinted or a reading is uncertain. Corrections are welcome —
see the [README](../../README.md#report-a-mistake-no-git-needed).
